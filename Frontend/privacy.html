Privacy Notice

This site is designed to follow the GDPR principle of data minimisation. We only use the information needed to let schools access the service.

In practical terms, this means:

  • We do not intentionally collect or store personal data in the application database.
  • School access is controlled by shared school login details for authentication only.
  • No unnecessary analytics, tracking, or marketing cookies are used.
  • Any temporary session information is used only to keep you signed in while the site is open.

If the service ever needs to change how it handles data, this notice should be updated to reflect that.
